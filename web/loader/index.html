1 · The break

Drop a break here or click to choose a file (WAV, AIFF, MP3, FLAC…)
A loop cut to whole bars works best. Stereo is mixed to mono.
Drag the green handles to trim the loop. Click inside the loop to hear a slice. The orange slice is the snare the fills use; shift-click a slice to pick another one.

2 · Send it to the FM-1

Connect the FM-1 running JUNGLE by USB, then press Connect (Chrome or Edge).

Playing it

PLAYStart / stop. With a MIDI clock coming in, it waits for the clock (MIDI start begins on the downbeat).
K1–K4HOME: chaos, evolve, pitch, gate · EDIT: shuffle, stutter, reverse, drop (what the chaos does) · FX: tone, drive, crush, swing
SELECT · ALGO · PRESETTempo · how often a fill comes (off, every 16/8/4/2 bars) · swing
White keysHold to play on from that sixteenth of the break (cue juggling)
Black keysHold: repeat 4 · repeat 2 · repeat 1 · roll · reverse · octave down · octave up · tape stop · mute · fill · straight
REC · SEQ · ARPA new pattern · CHOP (slices keep their pitch) or SPEED (repitched to the tempo) · fill now
GLO · SEL · OCT−/+Tap tempo · back to the break's own tempo · tempo −1 / +1
SAVEKeeps the knobs (the break itself stays in the flash on its own).
MIDI inClock, start, stop · notes 36–51 = white keys, 60–70 = black keys · CC 20–31 = the twelve knobs · CC 14 = tempo
